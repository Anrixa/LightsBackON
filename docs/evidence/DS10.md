## Scope implemented

1. Cabinet overlays on the two real C01 props (JW/JE). Their hinged door, interior and isolator handles take positions from `SessionHost.authoritative_state()` (repair stage and isolated grid junction).
2. Fuse carrier presence changes with the **accepted** open/remove/install stages. Broken east-trunk visual is concealed until the door is open and disappears after the replacement stage. Neither visual tries to infer the actual fault.
3. First-person meter body attached to the existing player camera. Its screen shows `? NOT MEASURED` until `MeterModel.readings()` contains a current inspection tick; then only known values may appear. Equipping another tool hides it.
4. Diegetic cabinet pings read `ping_added` / `ping_removed` events emitted by the host communications service; rejected or expired pings never generate or retain a marker. No extra RPC or local prediction.
5. Regression test methods in `tests/integration/test_DS10_fault_evidence.gd` cover noncolliding visual nodes, repair-stage changes, cut-trunk concealment, unknown meter readings, host-accepted inspections, and host-accepted ping/expiry.
6. No third-party assets, model files, textures, sound or addons added. Props are GDScript procedural meshes.

- Ping markers are ephemeral and do not persist across a restart.
- Current no-collision tests do not replace a human inspection of mesh occlusion, readability, shadows, contrast and handheld placement.
