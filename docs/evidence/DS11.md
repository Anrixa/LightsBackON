8. Add `tests/integration/test_DS11_temporary_supply.gd` (+ UID), with eight unit/integration test cases covering noncollision, anchor truth, host-connected cables, 4/6 normal, exact 7/15 and 14/15 progress and trip, grid causes unchanged by presentation, host-refused dual tie, and readable HUD.
