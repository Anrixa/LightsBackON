# EXP010 — Emergency Dispatch, Finite Logistics and Service Recommissioning

**Baseline:** `main` commit `1552d913adc2aa8c39163e16222b93111fd17ae8`
(merged EXP009, 19 November 2026). **Scope:** opt-in experimental expedition
source and headless host simulation. Human/controller/3D route and release
qualification remain explicitly **HOLD**.

## Changes and boundaries

- `scenes/expeditions/emergency_logistics.json`: 200 EXP01/EXP009-bound
  emergency manifests, 400 individual service cargo jobs, 200 supply depots,
  200 certification consoles, **800 unique authoritatively selectable targets**.
  The source includes eight domain-specific cargo pairs and three genuinely
  different operating patterns: 67 serial work orders (B depends on delivered
  A), 67 urgency/triage orders (either can be dispatched), and 66 crossdock
  jobs (A must take relay transport and B must take direct transport).
- Each manifest has exactly **3 spares and 3 fuel**. A direct shipment
  consumes 2 spares + 1 fuel, takes 6 fixed host ticks; a relay shipment
  consumes 1 spare + 2 fuel, takes 14 fixed host ticks. Both deliveries
  require deliberately mixed routes to remain feasible with the stock.
  A cargo can only be delivered at its own source-bound station after
  actual host travel time and the prior linked services' EXP009 certification.
- Work sequence:
  **host safety certified → open depot manifest → choose A/B and direct/relay
  dispatch → wait for authority-owned transit → receive at station → certify**.
  Stock, travel time, transit pause, dependencies and deadlines are never
  asserted by the client. If the player dispatches the wrong unreceived
  shipment, depot `recall` returns precisely its spent resources; replayed
  packets cannot recall/refund twice. Late delivery is recorded as mission
  quality evidence but never makes a campaign unwinnable.
- Actual unsafe upstream supply pauses shipments; a genuinely unsafe change
  following certification reopens the manifest and requires a host-safety
  recheck of installed cargo without spending consumables again.
- Source drift, job IDs, service link identities, foreign targets, allocation
  inflation, forced routes and duplicate levels fail inspection; the entire
  source set is audited independently by `tools/validate_exp010_logistics.py`.
- Each `operate_logistics` request passes the existing SessionHost wire
  envelope, member identity, content-revision, sequence dedupe, rate, payload
  shape, source target and host-owned range and optional physics LoS.
  EXP010 is included in the original authoritative state hash/revision/delta,
  with new opt-in content revisions and a source-owned 10 Hz tick after EXP009.
  Extraction requires EXP06, EXP07, EXP08, EXP009 and EXP010 solved.
- Source-only first-person stage includes target labels, a live budget/status
  HUD, `U` to choose direct/relay, `Y` to choose shipment A/B, `K` recall
  at depot, and `E` to interact. Physical accessibility remains **unverified**.
