## Distinct gameplay and source coverage

- \`critical_service_loads.json\` contains 200 source-pinned field boards,
  600 individually named service circuits and 1,000 unique targets.
  Each entry is pinned to its original EXP01 level/puzzle and the precise
  merged EXP010 logistics key. Source/cargo substitutions fail inspection.
- A board opens only after the live host has accepted upstream logistics and
  proven the fault, feeder and hazard states safe.
- Exactly **6 integer temporary PU** are available per board. Circuits use
  finite source-bound demands, host-owned stabilization ticks and deadlines.
  The 600 circuits cannot all be treated as cosmetic renamings:
  - **Cascade (67 levels):** 4/3/2 PU, dependency chain A → B → C.
  - **Triage (67 levels):** 3/2/1 PU, concurrent options and deadline
    evidence, with lateness recorded but no hard softlock.
  - **Interlock (66 levels):** 2/2/2 PU; A and B cannot be energized
    simultaneously, and both must be latched before C.
- An accepted \`energize\` command consumes live capacity. Fixed host ticks
  make a service stable. The player must intentionally \`shed\` at the
  endpoint after stability, freeing capacity and latching service recovery.
  Early shedding is recoverable and increments the abort counter.
- \`certify\` requires all three circuits latched, all temporary feeds shed,
  and upstream EXP010 safety intact. An unsafe upstream change reopens a
  certified board and moves previous latches to \`recheck\` without inventing
  power or reopening permanent saves.
- \`operate_balance\` is attached only to explicit EXP011 sessions. It passes
  the original SessionHost membership, transport identity, sequence/dedupe,
  rate, revision, content hash, payload shape, proximity and optional physics
  line-of-sight checks. The client's PU, host time, result and circuit state
  cannot be supplied in the command envelope.
- EXP011 appears in the canonical host snapshot/revision/hash barrier. Source
  content revisions are distinct from EXP010. All prior opt-out sessions and
  the certified 12-contract C01–C12 campaign remain unchanged.
- Experimental first-person stage includes host targets, capacity/state
  evidence, and E-to-operate commands. Physical access and real interaction
  remain **unverified**.
