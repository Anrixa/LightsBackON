## Authored development scope (do not overstate)

- Forty **explicitly authored individual site interventions** across five location regions (Alder Reach, Glasswater Quay, Iron Viaduct, Lantern Marsh and Slate Ridge). These are the first 40 locations, not 200 completed bespoke sites.
- The other **160 locations remain at A01's eight-family graph foundation**. EXP012 issue #197 stays OPEN; do not certify the 200 bespoke-location quality milestone from this PR.
- Each of the 40 cases explicitly names the location's intervention and stakes, uses its original atlas climate/risk/puzzle identity and five existing task IDs, and pins to its A01 worksite encounter key.
- Two distinct operating orders per site (priority / resilience): **80 authored route sequences** with 320 source-defined action steps, 120 new source-bound host interaction targets.
- Five mechanically implemented transition types: `hold` (start, dwell, confirm), `pulse` (narrow host-tick window), `sample` (two station readings separated by host ticks), `handoff` (second station action and original-site signoff), and `balance` (secondary trim followed by settling dwell).
- Failures have **step retry**, **previous-step rollback**, or **full restart** consequences per authoring source; each requires authorized recovery at the third site station. Early commit, missed host window, loss of supply, unsafe fault and route change are not accepted as success. Source replay provides safe paths that need no client-provided clock, resources or outcome.
- These are engineered interaction scripts in first-person graybox and host session. **They are not yet 40 separately produced final 3D level layouts or certified human-accessible encounters.** Future A02+ work remains necessary for all 200 genuinely finished places.
