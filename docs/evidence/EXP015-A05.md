## New host-level mechanics

A05 adds a second, *critical-service* assurance contingency AFTER successful
shelter recovery, rather than replaying the A04 shelter fault:

1. `assurance_assess`: require a still-valid A04 signoff and record whether
   original regional critical service is **really energized**.
2. `assurance_recharge` OR `assurance_conserve`: before the new fault,
   optionally exercise **real A09 one-shot** reserve recharge at the exact
   original reserve-anchor node. Recharge fails without both restored receipt
   and energized source or if the A09 tie/one-shot restrictions deny it.
   Conserving reserve always remains a valid non-deadlocking response.
3. `assurance_trip`: trip the original **critical-service feeder** through
   A09's real fault transition and original source-proven fault signature.
   This necessarily revokes the prior A03/A04 live-source proof until repaired.
4. `assurance_priority` OR `assurance_managed`: urgent critical-priority
   repair is available only when critical power was actually live at assessment.
   Resilience-first cannot claim verified critical service. Managed response
   records an explicit risk-class-specific local pressure, **not** a
   production balance, fabricated power, EXP04 field points or A09 reserve.
5. `assurance_clear`, `assurance_reset`: use the original A09 verified
   recovery procedure, clear source fault and safely reset the critical
   primary feeder; duplicate/reordered/forged operations are denied.
6. `assurance_verify`, `assurance_signoff`: require an actual safe critical
   feeder, the original A04/A03 signoffs to be live-valid again and **the same
   true original critical energization status** as assessment. A later
   fault/re-isolation invalidates `closed()` again. Only this opted-in phase
   adds a further extraction and EXP04 final restoration-task gate.

Source-derived branching across the 25 original topologies × 2 original EXP05
routes: 25/25 critical services live under the critical-first receipts,
0/25 under resilience-first. Under those same source receipts and prior
A03 buffer costs, the original A09 reserve anchor is energized/receipt-ready
for recharge in **3/25** critical-first scenarios and **0/25**
resilience-first scenarios. All other cases must conserve; these are
source-only paths, not physical game completions or utility-grid measurements.
