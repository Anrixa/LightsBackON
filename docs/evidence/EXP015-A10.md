## Mechanical change

The A09 original host source already records real `service_debt` from root
managed commissioning or later supply decisions. A10 gives each of the **25
original finale locations** (slot 8 in each source region) a new authenticated,
opt-in ledger resolution after A05 critical-service assurance closes.

- `settlement_audit`: read the *actual* A09 region reserve, service debt and
  EXP04-restored original source. The host, never an incoming payload, supplies
  these values. A05 completion is mandatory.
- `settlement_proof:<regional-risk-proof>`: distinct environmental clearance
  for flood, conductivity, mechanical, structural or exposure risk. Other
  strings are rejected by source identity.
- `settlement_pay`: request one real A09 `retire_service_debt` transition.
  This succeeds only if the original reserve anchor has its original EXP04
  receipt, is currently electrically energized through A09's real feeder
  graph, no standby tie is engaged, the host is at the original finale,
  and finite reserve is at least outstanding debt. It subtracts actual debt
  PU from reserve and reduces debt exactly to zero, once. No recharge.
- `settlement_defer`: retain the real debt and finite reserve unchanged.
  Mandatory when the anchor is dark or reserve insufficient; not a fake
  equivalent of a paid balance.
- `settlement_clear`: separate zero-debt path, no PU spent or fabricated.
- `settlement_verify` / `settlement_signoff`: dynamically compare the
  original ledger and source receipts. Subsequent ledger changes revoke
  closeout; extraction remains blocked until valid signoff.

All 25 source entries are pinned to original A09 finale, reserve-anchor,
critical-service and risk class, **125 already-existing EXP01 finale task IDs**.
No new rewards, saves, game coins, tasks, electrical sources or RPCs are
added. The prior SessionHost principal, sequence/dedupe, rate and range gates
are unchanged. Original `regional_recharge` cannot bypass A10 during
the A10 episode. The new final `enable_regional_settlement` parameter
