# EXP015 A12.6 — Regional Host-Dispatch Epoch and Cross-Dispatch Recovery

**Exact source parent:** A12.5 draft PR #251 at `13d55223359e429f431acd3b377852f174844f86`.

## Source defect closed

A12.4 bound original reserve-anchor signoff to authentic feeder/tie
mutations, but a completed finale's controller could remain valid
after the same process-local regional network was reattached to a
different/new host dispatch. On re-entry to that finale its old
source proof could be treated as current without fresh operator
verification.

- A09 `Network.current_dispatch_epoch()` is host-owned and
  process-local. The epoch starts at 1 after a verified first source
  configuration and increments **only** after successful
  `attach_site()` validation. It never changes power-event ledgers,
  reserve/debt, EXP04 receipts or production save formats.
- An opt-in A12 controller captures the network's current epoch when
  configured. `closed()` and source verification require the exact
  bound epoch and original finale identity. Commands sent through an
  obsolete worksite controller fail with
  `ANCHOR_DISPATCH_CONTEXT_EXPIRED`; resuming the same finale cannot
  resurrect an old signoff.
- The new finale dispatch starts with a fresh controller and the same
  original regional feeder and finite reserve/debt state.
- Original opt-in host content revision is `EXP015_A12_6_`.
  Inherited A12.2/A12.3/A12.5 expectations were updated accordingly.

## Evidence under test

- Real original A09 root SessionHost entry, followed by the original
  finale SessionHost and a second finale SessionHost, for **25 regions
  x 2 original finite-resource choices = 50 distinct scenarios**.
- Authentic EXP04 fixture receipts under the EXP05 campaign gate;
  foreign peer denial; original entry risk procedure and limited
  buffer/managed-debt decisions.
- Actual shared region network object and bounded reserve/debt
  conservation across the handoffs. Dispatch itself creates no
  electrical event, reward or new receipt.
- Region 11 and 25 upstream receipts remain truly missing, so only
  actual dark-source deferral can close; other original anchors require
  genuine trip, risk proof, clearance and feeder reset.
- Previous finale signoff invalidated by a same-finale new dispatch.
  Obsolete authenticated worksite intent cannot replay it. Failed
  cross-region attach cannot advance the epoch.
