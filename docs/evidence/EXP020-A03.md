# EXP020 A03 — collectible, loot and score feedback

Reuses the **existing** EXP04 task receipt and original per-level FIND
collectible. A lore pickup is source-authored, unique, and worth 0 points;
the original task kinds retain 40/55/70/35/85 field points. No invented
currency, new loot ID, or additional reward ledger.

Reward feedback is computed from *before and after a successful
Ledger.receive write* and only for validated original host receipt keys
newly committed into the account's profile. A resend/restart cannot
duplicate its feedback or award. A crash-recovered inbox may legitimately
contain other receipts; those are never displayed as current-session
awards. Foreign, stale, altered and uncommitted receipts fail closed.

An additive fifth tablet tab and live expedition field scoreboard display
the ledger values, score delta, and non-currency lore finds. This is
local save integrity, not proof of an adversarially unmodified file.

Source/headless qualification only. No 12-contract credits, economy,
persistent schema, item inventory or main-branch promotion.
