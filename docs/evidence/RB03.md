**The C01 solution matches the blueprint's known-valid shape.**
- The generator drives the pump only, never the pub, and the sign is never switched on.
- The solver records `E_TEMP_PUB` as a failing route (an 8/6 overload). It also finds that temporary supply is required: without
  it the search space is exhausted with no solution.
- The west fuse is repaired while the drain runs. The east trunk waits for the latched drain, then the generator is stowed.
- The solver differs from the blueprint in one place: it repairs the east trunk *before* stowing. That is valid and faster.

**Pillar checks.**
- The marker-only fixture is rejected with exactly `MARKER_ONLY`. A scripted player who only follows markers finishes it.
- The no-tradeoff fixture is rejected with exactly `NO_TRADEOFF`. Its 10 PU generator carries both routes at once.
- The storm fixture passes every pillar.

## Findings worth knowing

1. **Fixed-length waits made traces depend on crew size.** A faster crew reached the next repair before the drain latch had
   finished. Traces therefore use semantic waits ("until latch X", "until verified/solved", "until tick N"), and `harden()` inserts
   the wait a step depends on. The same answer replays at 1 to 4 players, so solo support is kept and crew changes only logistics
   time.
2. **Story contracts and the pillars.** Some story contracts fail the challenge pillars:
   - C04, C07, C09, C10 and C12 can be finished by the scripted marker-following player.
   - Only C01, C05, C06 and C11 have a temporary-supply tradeoff that the puzzle *requires*.

   These results are reported, not enforced:
   - the contracts are the tutorial campaign;
   - ADR-019 says their data does not change;
   - their real puzzles include optional objectives this format leaves out (C02 venues, C07 meter evidence, C08 uninterrupted diner).

   **For the owner:** if the campaign should also meet the challenge pillars, that is a content decision for the CT phases.
3. **The solver is complete within its limits.** `limit` (state or tick cap) is reported separately from `unsolvable`. The
   no-tradeoff check counts temporary supply as required only on `unsolvable`. A capped search therefore fails the pillar rather
   than passing it.

## Not verified / pending

- **Tier calibration is a design choice.**
  - The weights and thresholds in `rules.json` give C01 tier 2 and the storm fixture tier 4.
  - Whether tiers feel right needs people (D07/B studies).
  - Tier 5 is not exercised by any current challenge. RB04's generator will need harder candidates.
- **Not modelled:** positions, travel between stations, locks, tools, multiple players acting at once, and presentation.
  - Crew scaling is a work and carry percentage, not a simulation of four people.
  - The runner abstracts the host stack. It does not replace `solution_runner.gd`, and contract `solution.json` traces still replay
    through the host as before.
- **The marker-only check is a scripted proxy.** "Follows markers and holds Interact" is a fixed policy. Real players may find
  other shortcuts; playtests decide.
- **No location challenges exist yet.** No challenge ships under `content/challenges/<location>/`; RB04 adds Alder Street's 40.
  The shipped-set checks (par equality, pillars enforced, duplicates) are implemented but have only fixture data.
- **Out of scope:** nothing here touches Steam, the network gate, F02 or F06.
